📖 Reading: My First Day at a New School

My name is Emma and I am twelve years old. Last month, I moved to a new city with my parents. Everything was different for me because I had lived in the same small town all my life. I was excited about my new home, but I was also a little nervous about starting at a new school.

On my first morning, I woke up early. My mother prepared breakfast for me and helped me find my school bag. I put some books, a notebook, a pencil case and a bottle of water into my bag. Then my father drove me to school. The school was much bigger than my old school. There were many students walking around the building.

When I entered my classroom, I saw a girl sitting near the window. Her name was Anna. She smiled at me and said hello. We started talking about our favorite subjects. She liked science and I liked English. Our teacher, Mrs. Brown, came into the classroom a few minutes later and welcomed me.

During the first lesson, we introduced ourselves to the class. At first, I felt shy because I did not know anyone. However, the other students were friendly and asked me many questions. At lunchtime, Anna showed me the school garden. We ate our sandwiches together and talked about music, films and our favorite animals.

At the end of the day, I felt much better. I realized that my new school was not as scary as I thought. I had already made a new friend. When I went home, I told my parents that I wanted to go back to school the next day. It was a very good first day.

📖 Reading: The Importance of Learning New Skills

Learning new skills has become an important part of modern life. In the past, people often learned one profession and continued doing the same job for many years. Today, however, the world is changing quickly. Technology is developing, new jobs are appearing and many traditional jobs are changing. Because of this, people need to continue learning throughout their lives.

Learning a new skill can be difficult at first. For example, someone who has never used a computer may feel confused when they first start using different programs. A person who begins learning a foreign language may also feel frustrated because they cannot understand everything immediately. These difficulties are normal. The important thing is to continue practicing instead of giving up.

There are many different ways to learn. Some people prefer traditional classes because they enjoy studying with teachers and classmates. Others prefer online courses because they can study whenever they have free time. Videos, podcasts, books and educational websites can also help students develop their skills. The best method often depends on the person and the skill they want to learn.

Another important part of learning is practice. Reading about a skill is not always enough. People need to use the information in real situations. For example, a person learning English should not only memorize grammar rules. They should also speak with other people, write messages, listen to conversations and read different kinds of texts.

Learning new skills can also increase confidence. When people realize that they can do something that was difficult before, they often become more willing to try other things. A small success can motivate someone to continue working toward a bigger goal.

In conclusion, learning should not stop after school or university. Modern life requires people to adapt and develop. Whether someone wants to learn a language, use new technology, cook better or develop a professional skill, regular practice and patience can make a big difference.

Questions

1. Why is continuous learning important today?
2. Why can learning a new skill be difficult?
3. What are some ways people can learn?
4. Why is practice important?
5. Why should English learners speak with other people?
6. How can learning increase confidence?
7. What can motivate a person to continue learning?
8. Does learning stop after university?
9. What does modern life require people to do?
10. Give two examples of skills people can learn.

📖 Reading: Technology and the Future of Education

Technology has transformed almost every aspect of modern society, and education is no exception. Over the last few decades, classrooms have changed considerably because of computers, smartphones, digital platforms and online resources. Students are no longer limited to information provided by a single textbook or teacher. Instead, they can access thousands of educational resources from almost anywhere in the world.

One of the most significant developments has been the growth of online education. Students can now attend courses offered by universities and educational organizations located in different countries without leaving their homes. This has created opportunities for people who previously had limited access to higher education. Online learning can also be more flexible because students can organize their study schedules according to their personal responsibilities.

Nevertheless, technology does not automatically improve education. Having access to information is not the same as understanding it. Students must learn how to evaluate information, recognize unreliable sources and distinguish facts from opinions. The enormous amount of information available online can sometimes create confusion rather than knowledge. Therefore, digital literacy has become an increasingly important skill.

Another challenge is maintaining motivation. Traditional classrooms provide a physical environment where students regularly interact with teachers and classmates. Online learners may have greater freedom, but they can also become distracted or lose motivation if they do not have a clear study plan. Successful online learning therefore requires self-discipline, time management and consistent effort.

Artificial intelligence is another technology that may significantly influence education in the coming years. AI systems can provide immediate feedback, generate exercises and help students practice languages or other subjects. However, students must still develop their own ability to think critically and solve problems. Technology should support learning rather than completely replace the intellectual effort of the learner.

Ultimately, the future of education will probably involve a combination of traditional teaching and digital technology. Teachers will continue to play an important role because education is not simply the transfer of information. Teachers also motivate students, explain difficult concepts, provide feedback and help learners develop communication and critical thinking skills. Technology can make these processes more flexible and accessible, but human interaction will remain an important part of education.

Questions

1. How has technology changed education?
2. What opportunities has online education created?
3. Why is access to information not enough?
4. What is digital literacy?
5. What problem can online learners experience?
6. What skills are necessary for successful online learning?
7. How can artificial intelligence help students?
8. Why should technology not completely replace intellectual effort?
9. What role can teachers continue to play?
10. What combination may characterize the future of education?

📖 Reading Practice

Why Do People Travel?

People travel for many different reasons. Some people travel because they want to relax and escape from their daily routines. Others travel because they want to discover new cultures, meet different people or experience places that they have only seen in photographs. Travel can therefore be both enjoyable and educational.

When people visit another country, they often notice differences in food, language, traditions and lifestyles. These differences can help travelers understand that there are many ways of living in the world. A traveler may return home with new ideas and a different perspective on everyday life.

Travel can also help people become more independent. When someone travels to an unfamiliar place, they may need to find transportation, communicate with local people, manage money and solve unexpected problems. These experiences can improve confidence and decision-making skills.

However, responsible travel is important. Tourists should respect local communities, protect the environment and avoid unnecessary waste. They should also learn about local customs before visiting a new country. Responsible travelers can enjoy their journeys while reducing negative effects on the places they visit.

Questions

1. Why do some people travel?
2. What can travelers learn from other cultures?
3. How can travel increase independence?
4. What problems may travelers need to solve?
5. Why is responsible travel important?

🟢 Beginner Writing

Write 80–100 words about yourself.

You can talk about your name, age, family, hobbies, school, favorite food, favorite subject and future dreams.

🟡 Intermediate Writing

Write 120–150 words about this topic:

"Do you think learning English is important for young people? Explain your opinion and give examples."

🔴 Advanced Writing

Write 180–220 words about this topic:

"Technology has changed the way people learn. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using technology in education."
